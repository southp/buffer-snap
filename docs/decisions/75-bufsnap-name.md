# The app is called BufSnap

**Decision:** The public name is **BufSnap**: the display names of the app and the widget, what the app says, and the App Store listing. The subtitle stays "Hands-free ideas for Buffer". The bundle ID, the `buffersnap` URL scheme, the App Group, the widget kind, and the project, scheme, module and type names stay as they are.

**Why:** Nothing of that exact name turned up in the App Store or on the web on 2026-11-09, and it keeps the connection to Buffer that helps people find the app. Nobody sees the identifiers that stay, and changing them would mean new App IDs, a new sign-in callback and widgets that vanish from Home Screens.

**What it costs:** The name is close to Buffer's mark, so App Review could reject it under guideline 5.2.1, or Buffer could object. The owner accepts that risk. A rejection needs only a new name in the same places, and the listing says the app isn't made by Buffer.

Rejected:

- **BufferSnap:** contains Buffer's mark outright.
- **Thoughtdrop:** already in use at thoughtdrop.io, though not on the App Store.
