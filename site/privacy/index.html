BufferSnap Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

BufferSnap is an iOS app that captures your ideas and sends them to your Buffer account. It has no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your captures, until you delete them or the app removes older ones to save space.
  • Your Buffer sign-in tokens, in the iOS Keychain, until you sign out.
  • Your settings.

What goes to Buffer

When you sign in, you approve BufferSnap's access on Buffer's own sign-in page, and BufferSnap never sees your password. BufferSnap reads your account's organizations, so it knows where to save ideas, and sends the text of your captures to Buffer as ideas. Buffer's privacy policy covers what happens from there.

What goes to Apple

When you hold the mic button, BufferSnap uses Apple's speech recognition to turn your voice into text. Depending on your iPhone and language, this happens on the device or your audio is sent to Apple. BufferSnap doesn't keep the audio. Apple's privacy policy applies, and you can type instead if you'd rather not use it.

BufferSnap asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition the first time you use them. You can turn either off in iOS Settings at any time.

Deleting your data

  • Captures: delete them in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your iPhone.
  • Buffer access: sign out in the app.
  • Ideas already sent: these are in your Buffer account, so delete them there.

Children

BufferSnap isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Its history is public.
