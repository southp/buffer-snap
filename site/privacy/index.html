BufSnap Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

BufSnap is an iOS app that captures your ideas and sends them to your Buffer account. It has no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads, no third-party code and no tracking. Nobody behind BufSnap can see your ideas or your account.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your captures, as text, until you delete them or the app removes older ones to save space. The app and its widgets share them through a file only BufSnap can read.
  • Your Buffer sign-in tokens, in the iOS Keychain, only on this iPhone, until you sign out. They never move to another device, even through a backup.
  • Your settings, such as how long BufSnap waits before combining captures into one idea.

Listening

BufSnap listens only while you dictate: while you hold the mic button in the app, or after you press "Dictate an Idea" in Control Center, on the Action button, on the Lock Screen or on the Home Screen widget. A dictation stops when you press again, tap Stop, pause for 3 seconds, or after 60 seconds.

  • No recording is kept. The sound goes straight to speech recognition, and only the text is saved. BufSnap never stores or sends the audio itself.
  • In the background only while dictating. When you dictate without opening the app, iOS keeps BufSnap running for that dictation, and the microphone indicator shows. It doesn't listen at any other time.
  • On your Lock Screen. While you dictate from outside the app, a Live Activity shows the words as they're heard, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so anyone who can see your screen can read them. It goes away a few seconds after the dictation ends. It's drawn on your iPhone and isn't sent anywhere.

What goes to Apple

BufSnap uses Apple's speech recognition to turn your voice into text, in your iPhone's language. Depending on your iPhone and that language, this happens on the device or your audio is sent to Apple to be recognized. Apple's privacy policy applies, and you can type in the app instead if you'd rather not use it.

BufSnap asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition the first time you use them. You can turn either off in iOS Settings at any time.

What goes to Buffer

When you sign in, you approve BufSnap's access on Buffer's own sign-in page, and BufSnap never sees your password. Buffer may email you a sign-in link instead of asking for one. Buffer then hands the app a one-time code through a page hosted on GitHub Pages, which stores nothing. GitHub, like any web host, may keep standard request logs.

Apart from speech recognition and that sign-in page, Buffer is the only place BufSnap connects to, always over an encrypted connection. It sends:

  • The text of your captures, as ideas in your Buffer account. Captures made close together are combined into one idea.
  • Your sign-in tokens, to prove it's allowed to, and to renew them.

It reads only your account's organizations, to know where to save ideas. Buffer's privacy policy covers what happens from there.

No tracking

BufSnap doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't use advertising identifiers, doesn't collect analytics or crash reports of its own, and doesn't sell or share your data with anyone. iOS may send crash reports to Apple if you've allowed that in Settings.

Deleting your data

  • Captures: delete them in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your iPhone.
  • Buffer access: sign out in the app, which removes the tokens from the Keychain.
  • Ideas already sent: these are in your Buffer account, so delete them there.

Children

BufSnap isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Its history is public.
