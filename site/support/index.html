BufSnap Support

BufSnap turns an idea you say into an idea in your Buffer account, without opening the app.

Getting help

Ask a question or report a problem on GitHub. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you expected to happen.

Dictating without opening the app

Open BufSnap once and allow the microphone and speech recognition when iOS asks. Then add "Dictate an Idea" to any of these:

  • Control Center: swipe down from the top-right corner, tap + at the top left, tap Add a Control, and search for "Dictate an Idea".
  • The Action button (iPhone 15 Pro and later): Settings → Action Button → Controls → Choose a Control.
  • The Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and replace one of the bottom buttons.
  • The Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose BufSnap.

Press it and speak. Your words show on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It stops when you press again, tap Stop, pause for 3 seconds, or after a minute.

When ideas reach Buffer

Captures made within 5 minutes of each other become one idea, sent 5 minutes after the last one. You can change that time in the app, under the account menu → Batching, or tap Send now in the list. Without a connection, captures wait on your iPhone and go once you're back online.

Common questions

  • "Can't listen yet": open BufSnap and allow the microphone and speech recognition. If you refused them before, turn them on in Settings → BufSnap.
  • A batch shows "Not sent": tap Resend next to it in the list. If Buffer ended your session, sign in again.
  • "Buffer asked BufSnap to wait": Buffer limits how often an app can send. Your captures stay on your iPhone and go by themselves when the wait ends, and Sync now works again then.

Privacy

No recordings are kept, and there's no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
